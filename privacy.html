Privacy Policy

PDF Merge (pdfmerge.dev) — Last updated: August 25, 2026

Summary

PDF Merge is a privacy-first tool. All file processing happens entirely in your browser. We do not have servers that receive, store, or process your files.

What data we collect

We collect no personal data. PDF Merge does not require registration, does not use cookies for tracking, and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

File processing

All PDF merging is performed locally on your device using the pdf-lib library. Your documents are never uploaded to any server. This means:

  • No file content ever leaves your computer
  • No file metadata is transmitted
  • Processing works offline

Local storage

The site stores only a counter of completed merges and a one-time unlock flag in your browser's local storage to enforce the free usage limit. This data never leaves your device.

Payments

Payments are processed via the USDT (TRC-20) blockchain. To verify a payment, a transaction ID (TXID) you provide is checked against the public Tron blockchain. We do not collect or store any personal or financial information, and we do not require an account or email address.

Third-party services

The only external requests made by the site are:

  • TronScan / TronGrid APIs — to verify a payment transaction ID you submit
  • QR code rendering for payment addresses (local generation)

No third party receives your files or personal data.

Data retention & deletion

Since no personal data is collected or stored on any server, there is nothing to retain or delete. Your browser's local storage can be cleared at any time by clearing site data in Chrome settings.
